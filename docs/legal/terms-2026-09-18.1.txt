2. What Auralift does
The app controls Android media volume, audio gain and equalization using supported Android interfaces. Its minimum installation requirement is Android 8.0. Effect availability and audible results depend on the phone, media app and output route. Some apps bypass these effects. A setting of +35 dB is requested digital gain, not a promise of a measured 35 dB increase at your ears. Calls, alarms and ringtones are not supported targets. Auralift is not a hearing aid, a calibrated sound meter or a medical treatment.

3. Listening safety and controls
Start at a low system volume and increase adjustments gradually. Loud sound, particularly with repeated or prolonged exposure, can cause permanent hearing damage and tinnitus. Hearing damage may develop without obvious discomfort. Excessive output can also damage speakers or other audio equipment. Follow the listening and operating guidance for your device, headphones and speakers. Take regular breaks and avoid prolonged listening at high levels.

Reduce the volume and gain, or stop playback, if sound becomes uncomfortable or distorted. The absence of discomfort or distortion does not establish that listening is safe. Auralift does not measure sound pressure at your ears. Neither the displayed gain, the free-tier limit nor a warning acknowledgement guarantees a safe listening level.

Compare original temporarily removes Auralift's boost and EQ; Stop releases its effects. Stop in the floating player also resets gain and EQ and leaves the player open. Closing the floating player is separate from stopping boost. These controls do not stop your media player or mute other sounds. The sleep timer and its optional fade reduce or end extra boost; media continues at its normal volume. Android may delay timers when idle.

3. Listening and controls

Start at a low system volume and increase adjustments gradually. Loud sound, particularly with repeated or prolonged exposure, can cause permanent hearing damage and tinnitus. Hearing damage may develop without obvious discomfort. Excessive output can also damage speakers or other audio equipment. Follow the listening and operating guidance for your device, headphones and speakers.

Reduce the volume and gain, or stop playback, if sound becomes uncomfortable or distorted. The absence of discomfort or distortion does not establish that listening is safe. Auralift does not measure sound pressure at your ears. Neither the displayed gain, the free-tier limit nor a warning acknowledgement guarantees a safe listening level. Take regular breaks and avoid prolonged listening at high levels.

Stopping boost releases Auralift’s effects. Stop in the floating player also resets gain and equaliser adjustments. It does not stop the music or other media app playing. The floating player can remain open when boost is stopped. Closing the floating player is a separate action and does not, by itself, mean that every audio effect or media player has stopped.
